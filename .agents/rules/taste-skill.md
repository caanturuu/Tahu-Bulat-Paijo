# Tasteskill Rule: Anti-Slop Frontend & Design Taste

Apply these design principles whenever designing or implementing frontend UI, landing pages, websites, or components:

## 1. Brief Inference & "Design Read"
Before writing frontend code:
- State a one-line **Design Read**: *"Reading this as: <page kind> for <audience>, with a <vibe> language, leaning toward <design system or aesthetic family>."*
- Proactively avoid AI clichés: generic purple/violet gradients, dark neon mesh backgrounds, uniform 3-card feature grids, excessive glassmorphism, or bland default Inter font.

## 2. Core Dials (Default: 8 / 6 / 4)
- **DESIGN_VARIANCE (1-10)**: Layout experimentation and asymmetry (Default: 8).
- **MOTION_INTENSITY (1-10)**: Animation depth and micro-interactions (Default: 6).
- **VISUAL_DENSITY (1-10)**: Information density and whitespace rhythm (Default: 4).

## 3. High-End Visual Guidelines
- **Typography**: Choose distinctive, intentional typography pairings (e.g., editorial serifs, crisp grotesque sans, mono accents) suited to the brand. Maintain strong hierarchy and readable line-lengths.
- **Layout & Bento Grids**: Use asymmetric layouts, deliberate whitespace, variable card sizing, and clear visual anchors instead of repetitive symmetrical grids.
- **Color & Contrast**: Build curated palettes with intentional accent pops and high contrast. Avoid muddy default gradients and uncalibrated dark modes.
- **Micro-Interactions & Motion**: Subtle, physics-based, purposeful transitions (hover states, smooth entrances) that feel tactile, responsive, and performant.
- **Full Output Discipline**: Provide complete, functional code without lazy placeholders or truncated blocks.
